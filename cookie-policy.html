Cookie Policy

Updated: 1 October 2026

Cookies are browser files; local and session storage are related ways of retaining information on a device. The website loads Google Analytics, Google Ads, Microsoft Clarity and Tawk.to. Its current implementation loads these integrations when a page opens and does not provide a site-wide cookie preference panel.

Technologies used

The table combines cookies observed in a Chrome visit on 1 October 2026 with clearly identified provider-documented possibilities. Names, duration and availability can differ by browser, region, provider settings and interaction. Browser limits can shorten published defaults.

Website technologies and typical duration
Provider Names Purpose Duration
Google Analytics _ga, _ga_L2R09RPPTV Recognise browser visits and maintain usage/session statistics. Google documents a two-year default; the reviewed Chrome session showed about 400 days.
Google Ads _gcl_au and other _gcl_ identifiers where used Advertising and conversion measurement. About 90 days for the observed _gcl_au; other names depend on advertising interactions.
Google / DoubleClick test_cookie Check browser support for cookies. Short-lived; approximately 15 minutes.
Microsoft Clarity _clck, _clsk Recognise a browser and connect page views within a session. About one year (_clck) and one day (_clsk).
Microsoft services used with Clarity CLID, MUID, SRM_B, MR, SM, ANONCHK Identifiers and session functions associated with Microsoft analytics services. Observed: CLID about one year; MUID and SRM_B about 390 days; MR seven days; SM a session; ANONCHK short-lived.
Tawk.to twk_idm_key, TawkConnectionTime Maintain and coordinate the chat connection. Session.
Tawk.to tawk_uuid_* and chat local/session storage, where set Recognise a returning visitor and support the chat. The provider documents six months for its visitor identifier; session storage ends with the tab. Local storage can remain until removed.

Contact forms and external links

Web3Forms processes submitted form fields and messages for delivery. This is a data-processing service, distinct from the analytics cookies listed above. See the Privacy Policy . Opening WhatsApp, map or social-media links involves the destination provider’s own technologies.

Browser and provider controls

Your browser settings let you inspect, remove or block cookies and site storage. Blocking some technologies may affect chat or other functions. Clearing a browser cookie does not delete information already sent to a provider, and blocking cookies alone may not prevent every network request.

Google also provides an Analytics browser opt-out add-on and advertising preference controls . These are provider controls with their own scope. Contact the legal team for a request about information already associated with an enquiry.
